   whole-lifecycle spec-to-product OWNER can have.
4. A LATENT DESIRE users always wanted but settled without, because nothing did it.
5. INDUSTRY-RESETTING. After this ships, the category is redefined and we are the
   pioneers; competitors look like the old thing.
6. REAL, however complex. Buildable (name the hard research bet if there is one);
   no vaporware.
7. Leverage what ONLY Loki has: it autonomously BUILDS, RUNS, DEPLOYS, and OWNS
   the full lifecycle of many products across many users/repos, continuously,
